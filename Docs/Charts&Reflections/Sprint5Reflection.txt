Sprint 5, our final sprint, brought together all of the teamwork and technical skills we've developed throughout this project. After the productive momentum from Sprint 4, our improved 
familiarity with the codebase made tackling tasks feel more natural and efficient. We quickly worked through refining features like procedural level generation, enhancing the player
boosting mechanic, integrating the mini-map, and making interactive environment objects more polished and engaging.

Knowing this was our last sprint, we put extra effort into addressing any lingering technical issues and ensuring thorough integration testing. By staying proactive, we 
avoided the stress of a last-minute crunch and kept our workflow steady and manageable. Complex features like procedural content generation, connecting various levels, and fine-tuning 
player interactions demanded careful coordination. Clear communication was key, especially when finalizing interconnected systems such as entity management, sprite adjustments, and command 
logic.

GitHub Projects continued to be a huge help, keeping our tasks clear and allowing us to see progress at a glance. Frequent and thorough code reviews remained essential, helping us catch 
and resolve issues early such as sprite sizing errors, misplaced entities, or command logic bugs ensuring we were confident in the quality of our final deliverable.

Reflecting on the entire project, Sprint 5 highlighted just how far we've come, both as developers and as a team. Everyone contributed valuable skills and perspectives, 
which allowed us to create a polished and enjoyable game experience. Leo summed it up well when he said, "Looking back at our first sprint, it’s incredible to see how 
seamlessly we now collaborate—everyone anticipates each other's needs." This final sprint truly showcased our team's adaptability and effective collaboration. We're excited 
to carry forward the valuable experiences and agile practices we've refined into future projects, coursework, and careers.
